How it works

Install once, then forget it is there.

01

Download

Grab the latest z-tabs-x.y.z.zip from GitHub Releases and unzip it.

02

Load it

Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder.

03

Flip the switch

Click the Z Tabs icon and turn it on. Then open as many Zalo tabs as you like.

Under the hood

Zalo Web allows one tab and one chat socket per account. One tab becomes the hub and holds the only socket; the others talk through it.

Why Z Tabs

Small extension, no surprises.

Tab 1 Tab 2 Tab 3 Tab 4 1 connection

One connection, every tab

Every tab ignores the others’ “open in another tab” signal. The hub replays the handshake to each new tab and routes every reply to the tab that asked.

Nothing leaves your browser

No servers, no analytics, no logs. Z Tabs only runs on chat.zalo.me.

The hub hands off

Close or freeze the hub tab and another one takes over within a few seconds.

10 s

Safe fallback

If Zalo changes and no handshake arrives within 10 seconds, every tab falls back to plain Zalo Web.

5tabs open

Count at a glance

A green badge shows how many Zalo tabs are open, from three up, on the toolbar icon too.

Privacy

Your chats stay between you and Zalo.

Z Tabs keeps no logs and sends nothing anywhere. Your tabs talk to Zalo exactly as before, just through one shared connection.

No servers

Z Tabs has nowhere to send your data, and no account to sign in to.

No analytics, no logs

Nothing is tracked, counted or stored outside your browser.

Off means off

Turn it off and reload: you are back on plain Zalo Web.

Open source

Good to know

The honest limits.

Not an official Zalo feature

A Zalo Web update may break it. When it does, the 10-second fallback keeps your session usable.

One browser at a time

Zalo PC, or Zalo Web in another browser, still takes the single connection and disconnects every tab here.

Shared storage

Tabs share Zalo’s local database, so a conversation list may rarely need a reload.

Chrome and Edge

Needs Chrome or Edge 111 or newer. There is no Safari or Firefox version.
